# .impeccable

## Design Context

### Users
Academic researchers in AI + GIS, and the two collaborating professors (Xun Shi, Dartmouth; Wang's team, Tongji). They run structured community-health assessments over US counties — reviewing suggested factors, spatial data layers, methodology, and results, then editing and confirming each step. Used at a desk, in daylight, on a laptop. They are domain experts, not consumers; they value precision, legibility, and trust over flash.

### Brand Personality
**Precise · cartographic · grounded.** A scientific field instrument, not a chatbot product. Calm and credible; the interface should read like a modern survey/GIS console crossed with a clinical data report. It states what a step is and what it does, in plain language — never markets itself, never leans on "AI" as a selling point.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme:** light. Daytime professional use; data legibility first.
- **Type:** Archivo (industrial grotesque) for titles/labels; Hanken Grotesk (humanist) for body/UI; JetBrains Mono for coordinates, FIPS codes, and measured values. Explicitly NOT IBM Plex / Inter / Space Grotesk.
- **Color:** teal-forward (cartographic), OKLCH, neutrals tinted toward the teal hue. Ochre for "review/in-progress" states, moss-green for "confirmed", a restrained red for destructive/above-benchmark. 60-30-10 weight; accents stay rare.
- **Anti-references:** the "AI palette" (purple→blue gradients, cyan-on-dark, neon glow), glassmorphism, gradient text, colored left-border stripes on cards, templated icon-over-heading cards.

### Design Principles
1. **Plain language over AI language.** Say what a step is and does. No "the agent", "AI-recommended", "artifact" in user-facing copy. The system suggests; the analyst confirms.
2. **Data is the hero.** Coordinates, counts, and metrics get monospace and clear hierarchy; chrome recedes.
3. **State you can read at a glance.** The 8-step pipeline shows pending / suggested / confirmed / stale through weight, fill, and tint — never a decorative stripe.
4. **Honest placeholders.** Map/preview areas read as cartographic viewports (fine grid), not generic hatched boxes.
5. **Quiet motion.** One calm transition on step change; no bounce, no scattered micro-interactions.
